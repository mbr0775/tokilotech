Account deletion

Updated 6 October 2026

Request deletion of your Lankar account and associated data from Tokilo Technologies. You can make this request without installing or signing in to the app.

Request account deletion

Open deletion email draft

Or email mubassirnasar@gmail.com with the subject Lankar account deletion.

Send the email to request deletion. Your account remains active until support confirms completion. You do not need to reinstall the app.

How to request deletion

Email mubassirnasar@gmail.com with the subject Lankar account deletion. Send the request from the email address registered with Lankar and identify the account you want deleted. If you cannot access that address, contact the same support email to discuss account-ownership verification. Do not include your password, login token or password-reset link.

What happens next

Support verifies account ownership before deleting any data and explains any further verification required. Opening an email draft or sending a request does not mean deletion is complete. We process verified requests without unnecessary delay and confirm when completed. Contact the support email if you need a status update.

Data covered by deletion

Deletion covers the Lankar authentication account, associated profile, cloud cash books and their income and expense entries. Any information retained for a specific legal or security obligation is restricted to that purpose and explained when applicable. Copies in provider backups follow the applicable backup-retention cycle.

Copies outside the cloud service

Reports you exported, shared or saved outside Lankar are not removed from those destinations. Local device copies can remain until the app's local data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. After receiving deletion confirmation, clear Lankar's local data or uninstall it on each device you used. Save any reports you need before requesting deletion.
