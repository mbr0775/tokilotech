Privacy Policy

Updated 6 October 2026

This policy explains how Tokilo Technologies handles information when you use Lankar, our cash-book and budget tracking app.

Information used by Lankar

Account information includes your email address, account identifier and the name you provide. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides account details such as your name, email and profile image. Authentication credentials are handled by our authentication provider. Cash-book information includes book names, balances, income and expense amounts, descriptions and dates. You choose what information to enter; avoid putting unnecessary sensitive details in descriptions.

How information is used

We use information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, send confirmation and password-reset emails, save and sync cash books, calculate summaries and prepare reports. If you contact us, we use your message and contact details to answer questions or process requests.

Device storage and cloud sync

Lankar saves cash books, entries and pending sync changes on your device so you can use it offline. Account authentication and synced information are processed using Supabase. Currency and appearance preferences are stored locally. Uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account or synced data.

Service providers and sharing

Supabase provides authentication and database services. Google processes information when you choose Google sign-in. These providers may process data on infrastructure outside your country under their own service and privacy terms. When you export, save or share a PDF, you choose its destination or recipient; the resulting file contains the report information you selected. Tokilo Technologies does not sell your personal or financial information. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect accounts and the service.

Security

Connections to the configured cloud service use HTTPS. Access to your account depends on authentication and server-side access controls. Device copies and exported reports should be protected using your device lock and appropriate file-sharing choices. No system can guarantee absolute security; contact us if you suspect unauthorized account access.

Retention and account deletion

Account and synced cash-book data are retained while your account is active, until a verified deletion request is processed or the information is no longer needed to provide the service. You can request deletion from Lankar's Account deletion screen or the public deletion page. After identity verification, we delete the account and its associated profile, books and entries from the live service. Any information retained for a specific legal or security obligation is restricted to that purpose and explained when applicable. Copies in provider backups follow the applicable backup-retention cycle. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request and related obligations.

Your choices and contact

You can edit or remove cash-book entries through the app, choose whether to use Google sign-in, and request account deletion. For privacy questions, data-access or correction requests, contact mubassirnasar@gmail.com. Never send your password, login token or reset link. Requests may require verification of your account ownership.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through the service or another appropriate channel.
